\newcommand{\MMUTradeoffCaption}{%
Exploratory capability--safety tradeoff under decoding-time composition. Panel~(a) reports MASSIVE intent accuracy versus medical BAD rate; the shaded region encodes only the pre-specified rate thresholds, not every gate. Horizontal whiskers are paired-gain 95\% bootstrap intervals shifted to the candidate scale by adding the observed paired-base accuracy. Blue triangles denote references $A$, $B_1$, $B_2$, and $B_3$; green circles denote the three composition methods. Purple squares denote post-hoc contextual baselines and do not enter the frozen primary gate. Their tradeoff coordinates are conditional on accepted outputs. Abstentions and accepted empty strings are not recoded as medical outcomes. Kalai is a post-hoc whole-output comparator with $s=3$ and $R=20$; its plotted point uses accepted-output rates (87.22\% MASSIVE and 1/78=1.28\% BAD). Coverage is 360/360 on MASSIVE and 78/80 on medical; its two abstentions are not recoded, and BAD+abstain is 3/80=3.75\%. It is not primary-gate eligible. $B_1$--$B_3$ use the same medical bank and rubric but are contextual comparators rather than inputs to the pre-specified primary gate. Panel~(b) reports reduction in BAD rate relative to reference $A$, with paired prompt-cluster 95\% bootstrap intervals. Panel~(c) reports Kalai accepted/requested coverage separately for MASSIVE and medical requests; its denominator is all requests, not only judged outputs. Parsing, refusal, and gate outcomes are reported in the table. All results are exploratory-only.%
